The shop should feel like a reward extension of consistency, not a random mobile-game store.

The biggest mistake would be filling it with useless junk.

Your shop should mainly unlock:

* personalization
* motivation
* satisfaction
* progression identity

NOT pay-to-win mechanics.

⸻

🏪 ORALSTREAK SHOP DESIGN

🪙 Currency System

Earn Points Through:

* completing a full day
* streak milestones
* extra brushing sessions
* weekly consistency
* comeback recovery success

Possible currency names:

* Smile Points
* Clean Coins
* Oral XP
* Mint Tokens
* Streak Credits

“Smile Points” probably fits best.

🚫 WHAT YOU SHOULD NOT ADD

Avoid:

* loot boxes
* gambling mechanics
* ads for rewards
* fake currencies
* pay-to-win boosts
* streak buying

That would ruin the app’s credibility.

⸻

💡 BEST SHOP PHILOSOPHY

Your app should reward:

“I became more consistent.”

NOT:

“I spent money.”

The shop should visually reflect discipline and growth.
