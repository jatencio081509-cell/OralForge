ORALSTREAK — UI DESIGN SYSTEM

🎨 Overall Style

* Clean “health app” aesthetic (like Apple Health + Duolingo restraint)
* White / soft blue / mint green accents
* No heavy gamification clutter on main screens
* Everything centers around one action: complete today

Typography:

* Large bold numbers for streaks
* Simple sans-serif (Inter / SF Pro style)

🧠 DESIGN RULES (IMPORTANT)

* No clutter on home screen
* One primary action per screen
* No heavy gamification visuals (keep it subtle)
* Everything feels like Apple Health + Duolingo discipline mix
* Timer screen is sacred (zero distractions)
* Streak is always visible but not overwhelming
